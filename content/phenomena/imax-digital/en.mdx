## What you see

The same 1.43:1 picture is cropped top and bottom in one theatre and fills the screen in another.

## Where it sits in the chain

The 15/70 film frame is 1.43:1. See <PLink id="imax-film" />. Digital projection changes the projector, not the perforations on that film.

Digital IMAX in 2008 is two 2K projectors. The screen is about 1.90:1, so the top and bottom of 1.43:1 are cut off. The 2015 laser theatre is two 4K projectors, and that room can fill 1.43:1. 2K and 4K are the projector’s samples. The film frame has no such grid.

## How to tell

On a 1.90:1 screen, a 1.43:1 picture is black at the top and bottom, or already cropped. A full 1.43:1 screen is the laser theatre, not the 2008 dual 2K.

## Can it be removed

The screen is fixed. A 1.90:1 room leaves the top and bottom of 1.43:1 outside the screen.

## What to do

Keep the film frame apart from the projector’s samples. The film has no pixels. The projector is where 2K or 4K appears.

## Do not

Do not write dual 2K down as the resolution of 15/70.
