## What you see

The motion is even. There is no 2-3 hitch. Projection is a little fast, and the sound is a little high.

## Where on the chain

Fifty-field television makes 25 pictures a second. See <TLink slug="mains" />. 24 frames do not fit. The usual path speeds the whole reel to 25, a ratio of 25/24, about 4% fast, and the sound rises with it. Then 2:2 splits each frame into two fields from that same frame. There is no mixed BC or CD.

The picture rate of 25 decides this, not PAL's line-by-line reversal. See <TLink slug="pal" />. The path into 60 fields is the other page, <PLink id="film-to-60" />.

## How to tell

The duration is about 24/25 of the original. Frames do not repeat 2, 3, 2, 3.

## Lab

In 2:2 both fields come from one frame. Speeding the reel to 25 raises the sound. This figure does not draw pitch.

## Can it be removed

When the whole reel was sped up, putting 25 back to 24 restores the time. A cut that was finished at 25 and mixed with video does not step back cleanly.

## What to do

Confirm the speed-up is even, then decide whether to put it back to 24.

## What not to do

Do not explain this page with the 3:2 figure, and do not fieldmatch it.
