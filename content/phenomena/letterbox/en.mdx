summary: "The bars are already in the samples. They leave when you crop. Scaling up to fill cuts the sides, or stretches people."
---

## What you see

A black band above and below, or at the sides, with no picture in it.

## Where on the chain

The band and the picture are the same samples. Bars a player adds in the window, so the shape stays round, are on <PLink id="pixel-aspect" />.

## How to tell

Save the frame. The band is still there. Resize the player window and the band does not change its line count.

## Lab

There is no field animation for this one.

## Can it be removed

Crop to the active picture and the band leaves this frame. The picture is still there.

## What to do

Measure how many rows and columns the band takes, then crop. Filling a different shape means cropping further, or accepting a stretch.

## What not to do

Do not scale the whole frame taller or wider to fill. That stretches people, or cuts the sides.
