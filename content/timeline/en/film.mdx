---
title: "From the wide roll to four perfs"
summary: "The 1889 roll was slit into 35mm. Holes on both edges, four perfs to a frame, a picture about 24.9 × 18.7 mm, which is 4:3."
---

In 1889 George Eastman sold transparent film in rolls about 70mm wide. W. K. L. Dickson, in Edison’s laboratory, slit that roll into 35mm and perforated both edges. The Kinetoscope shown in 1894 used that strip.

The picture width is what remains between the two rows of holes, about 24.9 mm. One frame is four perforations. The pitch is 0.1875 inch, four pitches are 0.75 inch, and the picture height sits inside that stack, about 18.7 mm. Width and height were fixed together. The frame is 1.33:1, which is 4:3.
