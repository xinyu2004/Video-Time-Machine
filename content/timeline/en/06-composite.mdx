---
title: "Composite and component"
summary: "Dot crawl and rainbows are the subcarrier written into the spectrum. After decoding, both signals already share the samples."
order: 6
---

Composite adds luma and chroma on one wire and uses the subcarrier to keep them apart. S-Video (Y/C) carries chroma on its own wire. Component YPbPr or RGB never had that carrier.

Dot crawl, rainbows, and cross-color are leftovers of composite encoding. They can look a little like mosquito noise, but the source is analog modulation, not a macroblock. Once a decoder has turned them into samples, the carrier is gone. FFmpeg can attenuate chroma; it cannot unmix luma from chroma cleanly. See <PLink id="composite-leftovers" />.

Keep this apart from BT.601. 601 samples component video. Composite digital, at four times the subcarrier, is a different path.

After analog terrestrial shutdown, NTSC, PAL, and SECAM are no longer on the air. Countries switched the transmitters off in different years. Dot crawl and rainbows already recorded onto tape or into a file stay in the samples. Shutdown does not erase them.
