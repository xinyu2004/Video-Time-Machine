---
title: "PAL and SECAM"
summary: "625 lines make 15625 Hz. Adjacent lines average a phase error away. PAL-M and PAL-N use other subcarriers."
---

This station is color added onto 625-line black-and-white. The 625 lines, 25 frames, and 50 fields were already there, and the line rate is exactly 15625 Hz. PAL averages a hue error by reversing it on alternate lines. SECAM is the other color system on the same line count.

The color subcarrier is

fsc = (1135/4 + 1/625) × fh.

1135/4 = 283.75, and 283.75×15625 = 4433593.75 Hz. Adding fh/625 = 25 Hz produces 4433618.75 Hz. That 1/625 of a line is the line count itself. It lets the subcarrier drift against the line structure so the dot pattern is less visible. See <PLink id="composite-leftovers" />.

PAL inverts (R−Y) on alternate lines. Reflections off buildings, and weather and temperature changing the length of the path, make an NTSC hue drift, because the hue is the subcarrier’s angle against the color burst. The same phase error arrives at a PAL receiver. The decoder inverts (R−Y) back on the next line, so one line’s hue is positive and the neighbor’s is negative. A one-line delay adds the two lines: the hue returns to the angle that was sent, and the amplitude becomes cos φ times the original. Without the delay line the two hues stay opposite. Up close that is Hanover bars, one line after another. From far enough away the eye does the averaging. Colored dots that crawl along an edge are the other case. Brazil’s PAL-M puts that same line-by-line inversion on the 525-line System M raster. The color field rate is NTSC’s 60000/1001, so the color line rate is

fh = 525 × 30000/1001 ≈ 15734.266 Hz.

The subcarrier is 227.25 times that line rate, a quarter line below NTSC’s 227.5, so the inversion still falls in the gaps of the line structure:

fsc = (909/4) × fh.

909/4 = 227.25, and 227.25 × 15734.266 ≈ 3575612 Hz. Video bandwidth is only 4.2 MHz and the sound carrier sits 4.5 MHz above the picture carrier, so the European 4.43361875 MHz would land on the sound. The monochrome raster matches NTSC. The color does not decode.

PAL-N in Argentina, Paraguay, and Uruguay stays at 625 lines and 50 fields, so the line rate is still 15625 Hz. The channel is the narrow System N: 6 MHz, 4.2 MHz of video, sound at +4.5 MHz. The subcarrier moves to

fsc = (917/4 + 1/625) × fh.

917/4 = 229.25, and 229.25 × 15625 = 3582031.25 Hz. Adding fh/625 = 25 Hz produces 3582056.25 Hz. That extra 25 Hz is the same reason as in European PAL; 283.75 has become 229.25. A decoder looking for 4.43361875 MHz does not lock to this color.

SECAM frequency-modulates the two color-difference signals and sends them on alternate lines. It has no NTSC tint knob, and a PAL delay line does not decode it.
